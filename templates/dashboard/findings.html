4. Experimental Results and System Validation

4.1 Model Performance Comparison

Our experimental analysis compares the performance of multiple 3D reconstruction approaches, focusing on three key metrics: reconstruction accuracy, processing time, and resource utilization. The Depth Anything V2 model demonstrates superior performance in most scenarios, particularly when handling complex product geometries.

Comparative Model Performance

Key Findings:

The visualization reveals several critical insights about model performance:

  • Depth Anything V2 achieves 15% higher accuracy compared to baseline models, particularly in handling complex surface textures.
  • Processing times show an inverse relationship with model complexity, with more sophisticated models requiring additional computational resources but delivering higher quality results.
  • Resource utilization patterns indicate optimal performance thresholds for each model, suggesting opportunities for dynamic model selection based on product complexity.

4.2 User Interaction Analysis

User interaction patterns provide crucial insights into the effectiveness of different reconstruction approaches. Our analysis examines how users engage with 3D models generated by different approaches, measuring factors such as exploration time, interaction depth, and user satisfaction.

User Interaction Patterns

Interaction Analysis:

The heatmap visualization demonstrates distinct user behavior patterns:

  • Users spend 40% more time exploring models with higher detail preservation, indicating a correlation between reconstruction quality and engagement.
  • Interaction patterns show clusters around specific view angles, suggesting these perspectives are crucial for product understanding.
  • The temporal distribution of interactions reveals peak engagement periods, informing optimal model loading strategies.

4.3 Quality Metrics Over Time

Longitudinal analysis of reconstruction quality reveals important trends in system performance and highlights the impact of various optimization strategies.

Quality Metrics Timeline

Quality Analysis:

The temporal analysis of quality metrics reveals:

  • A consistent improvement in reconstruction accuracy over the testing period, with notable jumps following optimization implementations.
  • Seasonal variations in processing performance, possibly correlated with server load patterns.
  • Strong correlation between quality metrics and user satisfaction scores, validating our quality assessment framework.
